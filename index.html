Quick answers

Simple answers before checkout.

Condensed FAQs make the page easier to scan and keep the booking path visible.

Do guests need to pedal?

Railbikes use electric pedal assist and require light pedaling. Non-pedaling options are available through the Passenger Cart and speeder-style rides.

Where does the tour start?

All tours depart from Andrews Train Depot at 95 First Street in Andrews, North Carolina.

When should guests arrive?

Guests should arrive 30 minutes before the scheduled departure for mandatory safety instructions.

Is it good for families?

Yes. The ride selection can be positioned for active groups, families, rail enthusiasts, and guests who want a more relaxed cart experience.

What makes it different?

The route combines historic rails, mountain scenery, open-air travel, and a memorable stop at the Valley River Tunnel.

What should the website emphasize?

Lead with the visual experience, then quickly show ride options, route highlights, arrival details, and booking availability.
